IBA SYSTEM 15.0.0 patch 39  (small patch - copy over 15.0.0 patch 38)

HOW TO INSTALL
  Copy these 5 files over your 15.0.0 patch 38 (keeping folders):
    index.html
    js/app-chat.js
    js/app-workdesk-dashboard.js
    js/iba-assistant.js
    js/iba-vacation-handover.js
  Everything else stays as patch 38. (preview_patch39.png is only a picture.)

WHAT CHANGED
  1. Messages to yourself
     - Forward (Dashboard): you are no longer in the "Send to" list. If the
       task's Attention is you, it asks you to choose a person.
     - A message to yourself is refused everywhere.
     - The stuck "1" from the message you sent yourself clears by itself
       when you sign in (that chat was never listed, so it could not be read).

  2. Vacation hand-over safety (the Active Task count going to 0)
     If a profile still says Vacation = Yes (e.g. left on after a vacation),
     the hand-over used to move that person's open tasks to the replacement.
     Now a person who is working is NEVER treated as away:
       - not the person signed in on that computer
       - not anyone who signed in during the last 12 hours
     Only people really away (Vacation = Yes and not signed in for 12 hours)
     have their tasks moved.

  3. New Super Admin skill "Vacation hand-overs"
     - Shows who the system treats as on vacation now (and their replacement)
     - Lists the recent automatic hand-overs: who -> who, how many, which POs
     - Undo: puts the tasks back to the person (only those still with the
       receiver; ones already moved on or finished are left as they are).
       Each undo is written in the invoice history.
