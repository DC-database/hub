IBA SYSTEM 15.0.0 patch 40  (small patch - copy over 15.0.0 patch 39)

HOW TO INSTALL
  Copy these 2 files over your 15.0.0 patch 39 (keeping folders):
    index.html
    js/iba-assistant.js
  Everything else stays as patch 39. (preview_patch40.png is only a picture.)

WHAT CHANGED
  Invoice tracking (PO or vendor), phone and computer:
  - Every open invoice now has its own "Remind" button, next to INV and
    Mark urgent. It opens the reminder box right under that invoice,
    addressed to the person who has it, about that invoice only.
  - The button at the bottom of the PO is now "Remind all (N)" when the PO
    has several open invoices (one reminder listing all of them).
  (Before, there was only one Remind at the very bottom of the PO card, so
  on the phone it looked missing behind the invoices.)
