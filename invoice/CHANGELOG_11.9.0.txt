IBA 11.9.0 — Mobile Readability and Unified Page Titles

Corrective fixes
1. Mobile Logout
   - Logout is now visibly rendered before tap in WorkDesk, Inventory, and Invoice Management.
   - Existing logout confirmation and session-clear logic are unchanged.
   - The correction follows the app mobile-routing classes, so it also works when a phone uses Chrome "Desktop site" and reports a wide CSS viewport.

2. Mobile Invoice Records PO details
   - Vendor, site, invoice-count, metric labels, metric values, and balance values now use readable normal-state colors.
   - Readability no longer depends on hover/tap.
   - Existing card/status background, accordion behavior, calculations, searches, permissions, and documents are unchanged.

3. Desktop page-header cleanup
   - Removed non-essential labels such as WorkDesk Action Center, Inventory Stock Center, WorkDesk Records Center, Invoice Management, Bulk Invoice Entry, Records & Reports, and equivalent eyebrow/kicker labels.
   - Removed the incorrect WorkDesk wording that could appear while Inventory reused the WorkDesk page structure.
   - Standardized the main hero-title size, weight, spacing, and underline proportion across WorkDesk, Inventory, and Invoice Management.
   - Functional descriptions, counts, buttons, controls, and page behavior remain unchanged.
