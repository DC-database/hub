PATCH 13a - WHAT CHANGED

  1. Track an invoice - for everyone, NO key and NO AI needed
  - Route button in the assistant (also "Track invoice" chip, and the
    morning greeting for people without a key).
  - Type a PO number, or a vendor name; optional Site and Year.
      * PO number: straight answer - every invoice on that PO.
      * Vendor: if more than 10 POs, it first shows the counts by year and
        site ("Al Noor Trading - 12 POs: 2026 (3), 2025 (5), 2024 (4)")
        and you click one to narrow down. Nothing is read from Firebase
        for the counting (it uses the PO list file).
  - For each invoice: where it is now on the path
      Reception > Invoice Entry > SRV > Approval > Accounts > Paid
    who has it, how many days, since when, and its History (who changed
    it and when). Invoices still at Reception (job entry not yet in
    Invoice Entry) are shown too. On Hold / Unresolved / For Inquiry /
    IPC / PO Close Out show on the side (amber), at the stage they
    stopped. Read only. Each search reads only the POs shown (max 10).
  - Stages used:
      Reception: job entry New Entry / Pending
      Invoice Entry: Under Review, In Process, For Summary
      SRV: For SRV, SRV Done, No Need SRV
      Approval: For Approval, CEO Approval, Report, Report Approval,
                Report Approved
      Accounts: With Accounts      Paid: Paid
  2. Who sees what
  - All sites: CEO, COO, Finance, Accounting/Accounts, QS, Senior QS,
    Logistic(s), Procurement, any position with "Manager", the Super
    Admin and the vacation replacement. Everyone else: only the site(s)
    on their account (e.g. "177" or "175,176"). A PO of another site is
    refused with a plain message.
  - Amounts only for people who see amounts in the system today (Admin,
    Accounting, the vacation replacement, the Super Admin).
  3. The assistant for everyone (desktop only; phone unchanged)
  - Each person who wants to ask in plain words adds their OWN free Groq
    key in Setup. The key is kept in that browser for that person only:
    on a shared PC another user never uses someone else's key (your key
    from patch 12 moves to your name by itself).
  - For other users the AI only gets: their own brief / waiting list,
    Track invoices, find people, drafting. Open PO, New invoice and Show
    PO stay Super Admin only (refused even if forced).
  - Super Admin Setup: "Who can use the assistant": Everyone / Only me.
    Saved in Firebase iba_assistant/settings (see rules below).

FIREBASE RULES (database ibainvoice-3ea51, if not already added)
    "iba_assistant": { ".read": true, ".write": true }
  needed for "Only me" and for skills to follow you. Without it the
  assistant is open to everyone (the default) and skills stay in the
  browser.

PATCH 12 - WHAT CHANGED

  IBA Assistant - your personal assistant inside the system (Super Admin
  only for now, desktop only; the phone stays as it is)

  - A round gold-edged button at the bottom right opens a chat panel. Ask
    in plain words (English or Arabic). The gold dot on the button means
    something is waiting for you.
  - Every day at sign-in a short greeting: how many tasks and jobs wait,
    and the oldest PO. "Open brief" shows it without using the AI.
  - What it can do now:
      1. Brief / what's waiting - Active Task, Active Job, oldest items
         (with days waiting), PO Close Out counts. Taken from what the
         system already has in memory: no new download.
      2. Find and open a PO - vendor, site, PO value, invoiced, remaining
         and its invoices. Reads only that one PO. Click a PO number in any
         answer to open it on Invoice Entry.
      3. Fill the Invoice Entry form - e.g. "New invoice for PO 1002:
         B-300, 5,000, For SRV to Bob". It opens the PO and the form and
         fills Invoice No., value, date, status, Attention (first names
         work), note. It NEVER saves: the panel steps aside, the Add button
         glows, you check and press Add. It warns about a duplicate
         invoice number or an unclear name.
      4. Draft messages - reminders and follow-ups with the real PO and
         invoice facts, with a Copy button.
  - Skills: under an answer press "Save as skill" and give it a name (e.g.
    "Morning check"). The lightning button lists your skills; Run repeats
    the same steps WITHOUT the AI (no quota). "Ask AI again" re-asks it.
    Skills are kept in this browser and copied to a small node in Firebase
    (iba_assistant/<you>/skills) so they follow you.
  - The brain: Groq free plan, model openai/gpt-oss-120b (recommended).
    Brain switch in Setup (gear button): Google Gemini, Ollama on a PC, or
    any OpenAI-compatible service.
  - Safety: it cannot save, approve, delete or send anything. The key stays
    only in your browser (not in the code, not in Firebase). It sends the
    AI only what a job needs. If the free limit is used up for the minute
    it waits and tries again by itself.

ONE-TIME SETUP (about 2 minutes, free)
  1. Open https://console.groq.com and sign in (Google account is fine;
     no card).
  2. API Keys > Create API Key > copy it.
  3. Recommended: Settings > Data Controls > turn on Zero Data Retention.
  4. In the system press the assistant button > Setup (opens by itself the
     first time) > paste the key > Save > Test. "Working" means done.
  If Test says it could not reach Groq while the internet works, Groq may
  be refusing calls from a web page; then a small free relay (Cloudflare
  Worker) can be added in the next patch.

  4. Save one answer as a skill and run it from the lightning button.
  5. Sign in as another user: no assistant button.

          ".indexOn": ["remarks"]

PATCH 8 - WHAT CHANGED

  A. "For Approval" did not reach the task list  (js/iba-attention-picker.js)
  - cause: in the 14.0.0 layout the Attention box is a stand-in button. When
    Invoice Management was opened from the side menu, the real Attention
    field behind it had no names, so the name picked in the box never got
    into the save:
      * a new invoice asked "Please select an 'Attention' person" again;
      * an edited invoice was saved with an EMPTY Attention - it left
        everybody's Active Task. This is why PO 12345 set to For Approval
        with your own name did not show in your Active Task;
      * just editing a note of an invoice could wipe its Attention;
      * on opening an invoice, the box could show a suggested name instead
        of the invoice's own Attention.
  - now the box always shows exactly what will be saved, the picked name
    always goes into the save, and an invoice opened for editing keeps its
    own Attention until its status is changed. The automatic choices when
    the status changes (None, COO, CEO, Accounts, SRV person) work as before.
  - the save routine, statuses and approval rules are not changed.
  - Note: an invoice edited in the last days may have been saved with an
    empty Attention. In Invoice Records such an invoice shows no Attention;
    open it, pick the name, Update.

  B. Attention: type to find a name
  - click the box (or just start typing on it): a search line opens on top
    of the list. Type any part of a name, position or site ("mar", "ceo",
    "177"); the matching letters are marked; names that start with what you
    typed come first. Arrow keys + Enter pick, Esc closes, a click picks.
  - dark list in the popup style (was a white scroll list).

  C. Active Task and Active Job: live count that breathes, blinks and counts
     (js/iba-live-counts.js)
  - top bar: a pill "Active Task  N" (gold) and "Active Job  N" (teal) while
    something waits for you. It breathes (soft glow), its dot blinks, the
    number counts up / down when it changes. Click it to open the page.
    At zero the pill goes away. Below 1400px wide only icon + number show.
  - side menu: the number on Active Task / Active Job, a blinking dot on
    WorkDesk / Inventory, and the left edge that opens the menu glows.
  - browser tab title shows the total, e.g. "(3) System Login", so it is
    seen from another tab too.
  - phone: the Approve count (and the Invoice / Inventory switch count)
    breathes.
  - which tasks count: the same ones Active Task / Active Job already mark
    as yours (13.x side-menu rule): Attention names you or the person you
    cover on vacation, not On Hold; for inventory, the request waiting for
    YOUR step (source, approval, receiving).
  - updates by itself: your own small task inbox is listened to (Firebase
    sends only changes), the WorkDesk job list the Dashboard already keeps
    is re-checked every 15 s (no download), and only the newest 60
    inventory requests are listened to. Older inventory requests are
    counted from the last time Active Job was opened. Each time Active Task
    or Active Job is built (Dashboard start, opening the page, the existing
    refreshes) its exact number is taken.
  - all animations move only opacity / size, so the graphics card does
    them (measured: no extra page work); "reduce motion" on the device
    turns them off.

  D. Why it felt slow - found and fixed
  - one style rule added in patch 6 (popups) made the browser re-check the
    look of the WHOLE page every time anything changed a class (opening a
    popup, hovering the side menu edge, a tab, a row). On Job Records with
    600 jobs that cost about 39 ms each time (14.0.0 original: 0.03 ms).
    Removed (it styled nothing). Now 0.03 ms again.
  - Job Records "fade edges": on every page-wide class change it measured
    every cell of every row, even on other pages (about 15-25 ms each
    time). Same result now, measured once per row shape, only while Job
    Records is on screen: about 0.1-0.6 ms.
  - the notice (General Announcement) watcher measured the side panel
    after every change on the page even with no notice showing. It now
    checks first whether a notice is showing at all. Same behaviour.
  - approval print buttons: watches only Invoice Management, once per
    frame (was the whole page).
  - no live blur behind small controls (search boxes, buttons, chips,
    tabs) - the graphics card redrew each one on every scroll / hover. The
    big glass panels, cards, side panel and top bar keep their blur.
  - measured (1600x950, 600 jobs, 500 invoices, 900 items), cost of one
    page-wide class change:
        page            14.0.0   patch 7   patch 8
        Dashboard        0.02     3.35      0.04 ms
        Active Task      0.04     0.87      0.04 ms
        Job Records      0.03    39.36      0.03 ms
        Material Stock   0.02     0.39      0.02 ms
        Invoice Records  0.03     0.57      0.03 ms

  E. Batch Entry: "Global Overrides" right under the Search Note line
  - the page rows shared the spare height of the window, which pushed
    Global Overrides far down. The list row now takes the spare height.

  F. Previous version  (login screen + Settings)
  - login (desktop and phone): "Use the previous version" under the box.
  - Settings: "Previous Version" card with "Open previous version".
  - both open https://port.iba.com.qa/invoice-v1/ in the same tab (only one
    IBA tab can be open at a time, so it replaces this one). Same data and
    same login: someone signed in here is signed in there too.
  - the new version stays the main one: https://port.iba.com.qa/invoice/
  - to set it up: put the old build (all its files) in a folder
