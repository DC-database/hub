==================== PATCH 5 (included) ====================

  A. One approval routine (desktop and phone)
  - Anyone named in the Attention of a "For Approval" invoice can approve or
    reject it, whatever their position. With several names in Attention, each
    of them can act (the first decision sends it back). A vacation stand-in
    can act for the person they cover.
  - "CEO Approval" invoices: the CEO named in Attention approves them; they
    now also show in the CEO's Active Task ("Make Approval" opens the same
    approve / reject window).
  - Approve: the invoice gets its own ESN at once (same format as before:
    5 letters + 5 digits / NAME; CEO 5 + 6 / NAME). It is registered in
    Firebase: manager_approved/esn_registry/<ESN> (who, PO, invoice, slot)
    and manager_approved/<PO>_<invoice> (esn_1..3 + CEO slot).
  - Reject: a note is required. No ESN.
  - After the decision the invoice goes back to the sender: Irwin, or his
    replacement while he is on vacation.
  - Up to 3 approvals per invoice (slots 1-3, any name takes the next free
    slot). The CEO has his own slot on top of the 3.
    * a 4th approval is refused ("This invoice already has 3 approvals");
      before, it overwrote the 3rd ESN
    * setting "For Approval" on an invoice that already has 3 approvals is
      blocked (Invoice Entry, Active Task, Batch Entry, Summary Note)
  - The daily approval code (approval_sequences, e.g. 20261007.0003) is no
    longer created. The ESN is the only approval number. Old decisions that
    have a daily code still print with it.
  - The invoice's own note is kept; the approver's note is saved with the
    decision (and in the history).
  - WhatsApp stays optional: after approving, "Send to WhatsApp (optional)"
    sends the ESN list as a second proof.

  B. Sender's Active Task: new "Approved" and "Rejected" tabs
  - Each returned invoice waits here, with "Approved by <name> · ESN <ESN> —
    <note>" (or "Rejected by <name> — <note>") in the Note column.
  - Print (approved): prints every approval of the invoice with its ESN as
    stamps (side by side) on the Site Approval area of an A4 page. It prints
    from its own small page, so no other screen gets printed with it.
  - Process: opens the invoice in Invoice Entry to move it to the next step
    (for example CEO Approval, or For Approval again after a rejection).
    Once its status changes, it leaves the Approved / Rejected tab.
  - Invoice Records: the print button on an approved / rejected invoice now
    prints the ESN stamps.

  C. Phone workspace (nothing from the desktop on the phone)
  - No side menu, no Dashboard, no desktop pages behind it (the phone no
    longer downloads the Dashboard data either).
  - Header: IBA mark, your name, refresh, and an Invoice | Inventory switch
    with only the sides you use (Invoice: when you have Invoice Records
    access or an invoice is waiting for you; Inventory: everyone).
  - Invoice side
    * Approve: invoices waiting for you (For Approval; CEO Approval for the
      CEO). Tick boxes + "Approve selected", or Approve / Reject per card.
      Every card has View PDF and shows "n of 3" approvals used. The amount
      cannot be changed on the phone. Reject asks for a note.
      After approving: list of ESNs + optional WhatsApp.
    * Records (admins, Irwin, vacation stand-in - same access as before):
      search PO, vendor or invoice no. + status; view only.
  - Inventory side
    * Approve (admins = the people the transfer form allows as Approver, or
      their vacation stand-in): Transfer / Restock / Usage / Return waiting
      at "Pending Admin" for you. Tick boxes + "Approve selected", or one by
      one. The quantity can be lowered (not raised). Reject asks for a note.
      Each request is re-read first; if someone already moved it on, it is
      not touched ("Already processed").
    * Item search: the existing Item Finder (search, scan, photo, stock,
      movement history, create transfer request), now dark.
  - Messages and Logout on every side.
  - Chrome "Desktop site" mode on a phone still opens the full desktop.

  Not changed: inventory desktop approvals, stock logic, ESN formats, access
  rules for Records / Invoice Entry, everything else from patch 1-4.
